Frequently asked questions

  • Where does the tide data come from? Tide predictions are sourced from the U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA).
  • Can I use Just Tides for navigation? No. Just Tides is a tide reference for general use and should not be relied on for navigation or safety-critical decisions.
  • How do I change stations? Tap the station name to search by name, or allow location access to automatically find the nearest station. Tap the star to save favorites.
  • What are the tips in the app? Tips are optional and support development. They don't unlock features, and you won't receive any physical or digital goods.
  • Why are my tide times slightly different from another source? Predictions can vary by station, datum, and data source. Just Tides shows NOAA high/low predictions for the selected station.
